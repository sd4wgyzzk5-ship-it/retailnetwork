WHO WE ARE

About Retail Network

Retail Network is a digital media and networking initiative focused on connecting retail professionals, sharing real career journeys and giving founders a platform to tell their stories.

Through interviews and short-form videos, we aim to inspire people and build meaningful connections across the retail community.

Our Mission

To connect people, amplify voices and make real-world experience more accessible.

Our Vision

A global community where retail professionals, entrepreneurs and business leaders learn from one another.

THE JOURNEY

Our Story

Retail Network began with zero shortcuts: late-night conversations with retail workers after their shifts. Those rooftop and street-side interviews grew into a global platform connecting founders and business leaders.

Humble BeginningsAt midnight, Hammud respectfully asked retail employees finishing their shifts to share their stories.

Built Through ResilienceRooftop and street-side interviews grew from a passion for content creation and human connection.

A Global PlatformRetail Network now connects founders and business leaders from around the world.

BEHIND THE PLATFORM

Meet the Founder

HAMMUD AL MAHIM

Founder & CEO, Retail Network

Balancing a demanding career as a seaman in the shipping line while fueling an absolute passion for content creation, Hammud Al Mahim built Retail Network from the ground up through sheer grit. The journey began with incredibly humble and difficult roots—waiting by the bins late at night around midnight when retail employees finished their shifts, respectfully requesting them to share their stories. From those late-night rooftop and street-side interviews to connecting global founders, this platform is built on absolute resilience and a deep love for human connection.
